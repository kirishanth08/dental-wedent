Root Canal Treatment

Root canal treatment saves severely damaged or infected teeth by removing the diseased pulp tissue and sealing the tooth to prevent further infection. Modern techniques make the procedure comfortable and typically completed in just one or two visits.

When the inner pulp of a tooth becomes inflamed or infected due to deep decay, cracks, or trauma, a root canal is the best way to preserve the tooth rather than extract it. This endodontic procedure removes the source of pain and infection while maintaining your natural tooth structure.

  • Saves your natural tooth
  • Eliminates tooth pain
  • Prevents spread of infection
  • No extraction needed
  • Restores full chewing function
  • Long-lasting results
  • Quick recovery time
  • Preserves your natural smile

The Root Canal Process

After numbing the area with local anesthesia, we isolate the tooth with a rubber dam for a clean working environment. We create a small opening in the crown of the tooth to access the infected pulp chamber. Using specialized micro-instruments, we carefully remove the damaged pulp, clean and shape the root canals, and disinfect the interior space.

The canals are then filled with a biocompatible gutta-percha material and sealed. Finally, we place a temporary or permanent restoration to protect the tooth.

What to Expect

A root canal procedure typically takes 60 to 90 minutes and is no more uncomfortable than getting a standard filling. Thanks to modern anesthesia and techniques, most patients report little to no pain during the treatment. The discomfort that prompted the procedure usually subsides immediately after.

After the root canal, your tooth may feel slightly sensitive for a few days, which can be managed with over-the-counter pain relievers. We recommend avoiding chewing on the treated tooth until the final restoration is placed. With proper care, a root canal-treated tooth can last a lifetime, making it a cost-effective alternative to extraction and replacement.
