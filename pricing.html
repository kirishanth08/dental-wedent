Our pricing plans are structured as monthly subscriptions that cover a bundle of preventive and restorative dental services. Each plan tier builds on the previous one, adding more advanced treatments and exclusive perks. You can choose to pay monthly or switch to annual billing to save 20%.

Yes, WeDent accepts most major dental insurance plans. Our front desk team will verify your benefits before your first visit and handle all insurance claims on your behalf.
