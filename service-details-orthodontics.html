Orthodontics (Braces)

Orthodontic treatment straightens crooked teeth, corrects bite issues, and creates a beautifully aligned smile. We offer both traditional braces and modern clear aligners to suit every lifestyle and treatment need.

A properly aligned bite is not just about aesthetics — it also improves oral function, reduces strain on jaw joints, and makes it easier to maintain good oral hygiene. Whether you're a child, teen, or adult, orthodontic treatment can transform your smile and improve your overall dental health.

  • Straighter, more aligned teeth
  • Corrects overbite and underbite
  • Improves chewing and speech
  • Reduces risk of tooth damage
  • Prevents uneven wear on teeth
  • Boosts self-confidence
  • Multiple options available
  • Customized treatment plans
Book a Consultation

Treatment Options

We begin with a comprehensive evaluation including digital X-rays, 3D scans, and photographs to map out your ideal treatment plan. For traditional braces, we bond metal or ceramic brackets to each tooth and connect them with archwires that gradually shift teeth into alignment.

For patients preferring discretion, we offer clear aligner systems like Invisalign, where you wear a series of custom-made, virtually invisible trays that are replaced every two weeks. Both options require periodic check-ups every 4-6 weeks to monitor progress and make adjustments.

What to Expect

The duration of orthodontic treatment varies depending on the complexity of your case, typically ranging from 12 to 24 months. During treatment, you may experience mild discomfort or pressure after each adjustment, which subsides within a few days. We provide guidance on dietary modifications and oral hygiene practices to keep your teeth healthy throughout treatment.

After active treatment is complete, you will transition to the retention phase, wearing a retainer to maintain your new smile alignment. We offer both fixed and removable retainer options and schedule regular follow-up visits to ensure your results remain stable for years to come.
