Dental Cleaning

Professional teeth cleaning removes plaque, tartar, and surface stains that daily brushing can't address. Our hygienists use advanced ultrasonic tools and polishing techniques to leave your teeth feeling smooth, fresh, and visibly brighter.

Regular dental cleanings are one of the most important steps you can take to maintain your oral health. Even with diligent brushing and flossing at home, hard-to-reach areas accumulate plaque that hardens into tartar, which can only be removed with professional instruments.

  • Removes harmful plaque and tartar buildup
  • Prevents cavities and gum disease
  • Fresher breath and cleaner mouth
  • Brighter, more polished smile
  • Early detection of oral health issues
  • Non-invasive and completely painless
  • Recommended every 6 months
  • Boosts overall oral hygiene

The Cleaning Process

Our dental cleaning begins with a thorough examination of your mouth to check for any signs of cavities, gum disease, or other concerns. Using an ultrasonic scaler, we gently remove hardened tartar deposits from above and below the gumline.

Next, we polish your teeth with a high-speed brush and flavored paste to eliminate surface stains and create a smooth, shiny finish. Finally, we floss between all teeth and apply a fluoride treatment to strengthen enamel and protect against decay.

What to Expect

A standard dental cleaning takes approximately 45 minutes to an hour and is generally painless. You may feel some pressure as the hygienist works, but the procedure should not cause discomfort. Most patients leave their cleaning appointment with teeth that feel noticeably smoother and cleaner.

We recommend scheduling a professional cleaning every six months to maintain optimal oral health. Regular cleanings help prevent the buildup of plaque and tartar, reduce your risk of cavities and gum disease, and allow us to catch potential issues early before they become more serious and costly to treat.
