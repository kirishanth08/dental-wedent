Teeth Whitening

Professional teeth whitening is a safe, effective way to brighten your smile by several shades in just one visit. Unlike over-the-counter products, our in-office treatment delivers dramatic, long-lasting results under expert supervision.

Over time, teeth naturally become discolored from food, drinks, tobacco, and aging. Professional whitening penetrates beyond the surface to break down deep stains, restoring a bright, youthful appearance that boosts your confidence and leaves a lasting impression.

The Whitening Process

Before whitening, we perform a thorough cleaning to remove any surface debris. We then apply a protective gel to your gums to shield them from the bleaching agent. A professional-grade hydrogen peroxide gel is carefully applied to each tooth and activated with a specialized LED light.

This process is repeated in three 15-minute cycles, with fresh gel applied each time. After the final cycle, we apply a desensitizing agent and a fluoride rinse to minimize any post-treatment sensitivity.

What to Expect

The entire whitening session takes approximately 60 to 90 minutes. You may experience some temporary tooth sensitivity following the procedure, which typically subsides within 24 to 48 hours. Our advanced whitening formula is designed to minimize sensitivity while maximizing brightness.

To maintain your results, we recommend avoiding stain-causing foods and beverages for 48 hours after treatment and maintaining good oral hygiene. With proper care, professional whitening results can last one to two years or longer. We also offer take-home touch-up kits to help you maintain your bright smile.
