Dental Fillings

Dental fillings repair cavities and restore damaged teeth to their original shape and function. We use tooth-colored composite resin materials that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth, providing both durability and aesthetics.

When decay damages a tooth, a filling is the most effective way to restore its structure and prevent further deterioration. Our composite resin fillings are carefully color-matched to your surrounding teeth, making them virtually invisible while providing a strong, lasting restoration.

  • Restores tooth shape and function
  • Tooth-colored and virtually invisible
  • Prevents further decay
  • Quick and straightforward procedure
  • Durable and long-lasting
  • Preserves natural tooth structure
  • Completed in a single visit
  • Maintains your natural smile

The Filling Process

We begin by administering local anesthesia to ensure complete comfort. Using a dental drill or laser, we remove the decayed portion of the tooth, leaving only healthy tooth structure. The cavity is then cleaned and disinfected to eliminate any remaining bacteria.

We apply a bonding agent and carefully layer the composite resin material, shaping it to match your tooth's natural contours. A specialized curing light hardens each layer in seconds. Finally, we polish the filling to a smooth finish that blends perfectly with your surrounding teeth.

What to Expect

A dental filling is one of the most common and straightforward dental procedures, typically completed in 30 to 60 minutes. The local anesthesia ensures you feel no pain during the treatment, though you may feel some vibration and pressure as the decayed material is removed.

After the anesthesia wears off, your filling should feel completely natural. Composite resin fillings bond directly to your tooth, providing excellent strength and durability. With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, your filling can last many years. If you experience sensitivity or discomfort after the procedure, it typically resolves within a few days.
